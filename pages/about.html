Why GearVault exists and how we elevate hardware shopping in Pakistan

Chapter 01 • The Spark

Solving Hardware Guesswork

Gamers and creators in Pakistan frequently struggle with outdated spec sheets, hidden exchange markups, and costly socket or RAM mismatches when sourcing PC components online.

GearVault (Section SE-B, Group 08) was conceived to replace cluttered spreadsheets with an interactive dark-mode storefront where every CPU, GPU, motherboard, and peripheral includes verified specs, real-world PKR pricing, and instant compatibility insights.

Chapter 02 • Our Standard

Precision, Thermals & Trust

Whether you are picking up an RTX 4070 Super on Flash Deals or assembling an AM5 Ryzen 7800X3D workstation from scratch, every flow in GearVault is designed around clarity and speed.

Our platform integrates dynamic catalog filtering, live countdown drops, PSU wattage estimation, side-by-side gear comparison, and instant local cart & wishlist persistence with zero backend friction.
